# DFT and exchange-correlation functionals

Kohn-Sham density-functional theory (DFT) describes the electronic problem through the electron density plus an exchange-correlation (XC) approximation.

The functional is part of the method identity. LDA, PBE, r2SCAN, PBE0, B3LYP, and range-separated hybrids make different approximations.

Broad families include LDA, GGA, meta-GGA, hybrid, and range-separated hybrid functionals. Composite methods may also include dispersion or other corrections; those terms are part of the named method when required.
